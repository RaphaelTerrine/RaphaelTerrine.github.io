I am a third year PhD student at [CMAP](https://portail.polytechnique.edu/cmap/en), École polytechnique and at [UMA](https://uma.ip-paris.fr/). I am supervised by [Philippe Moireau](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~philippe.moireau/), from CMAP, and by [Laurent Bourgeois](https://cv.hal.science/laurent-bourgeois), from UMA.

I am interested in the study of Partial Differential Equations both numerically and theorically. My work primarily focuses on the study of inverse problems and on the numerical analysis of the methods employed. Currently, my research is devoted to tsunami detection through free-surface measurements.
